## No AI Slop

The editorial pass adapts the editing principles, pattern checks, and evaluation guidance from **No AI Slop by Peter Yang**. The upstream files are available at [the pinned source revision](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop/tree/000650b156983f5159695b441477f4e63b25dc85).

Upstream revision: `000650b156983f5159695b441477f4e63b25dc85`.

Adaptations for Work to Post include using the draft and profile already supplied, applying editing quietly, omitting automatic change reports, preserving precise technical terms, and rechecking evidence and length after editing. The upstream originals are retained in the source repository under `third_party/no-ai-slop` for traceability. The applicable license is reproduced below and included with distributed copies.
