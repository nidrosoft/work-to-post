# A brief for one post

Use this for each new idea. Paste rough notes instead if that is easier.

```text
What happened, what I built, or what I noticed:
Who should care, and why:
What I can support with facts, examples, or an artifact:
What I want the reader to do or understand:
```

Add these only when they matter:

```text
Primary outcome: CLIENTS / OFFERS / help or discussion with no direct ask
My part in the work:
Topic and specific use case:
Angle preference, or choose for me:
Source text or links, with dates and measurement conditions:
Facts versus interpretation versus a future plan:
Known limitations or counterexamples:
Available image, recording, code, or demo:
Format and length:
Account capability, if not already in my profile:
Purpose: publication draft / private illustrative preview
Details that must stay private:
```

Reusable request:

> Use Work to Post in Draft mode. Use my profile and the notes below. Choose the strongest supported angle for this reader. Give me the post, its supporting material, and any facts or assets still needed. Do not add achievements or results I have not supplied.

If you do not yet have a project result, bring a real question, an observation, a source to interpret, a diagnostic, or a decision you can explain. The assistant should select an angle that fits that material.
