# Your reusable profile

Fill this once and update it when your positioning changes. Rough notes work. Leave unknowns blank. This is personal working context, so keep your completed copy outside a public framework repository.

```text
Preferred delivery: short post / long single post / thread / choose for the material
Real availability or capacity, if relevant:

Sustainable posting capacity:
```

For a quick start, supply only what you do, who you want to reach, and one piece of work or knowledge. The assistant can infer a provisional profile and identify important gaps. A job title alone does not tell it what you can prove.

Possible topic families include product engineering, frontend, backend and full-stack software, AI architecture, design and Figma, founder operations, small-business consulting, and forward-deployed engineering. Choose the areas you actually know. The framework does not require a person to cover all of them.
